The token system and anchor components that everything else is built from. Calm by construction — the interface is meant to disappear.

Foundation

Color

Warm greige grounds — never pure white or black. One signature hue, Heath, carries “the present” and the system’s own voice. Amber is the only signal color; it means slipping, never danger. Hex values update live with the theme.

Foundation

Typography

A serif voice for the household’s own quiet statements, a humanist sans for everything operational, and tabular figures for anything that counts. No display face shouting — a loud one would fight the calm.

Foundation

Space, radius & elevation

An 8-pt base with a deliberately large rhythm at the surface level — air is the primary material. Soft, warm shadows, lit from above like a lamp; never hard black drop-shadows.

Component · anchor

The one thing

The default surface shows a single responsibility, not a list — the Balancer and Scheduler decide what matters most, right now, for you. Complete it and it clears; the next thing rises into its place.

Not a task — a thing that’s yours to make sure happens. It carries the cognitive owner (who remembers) separately from the doer (who does). Five states; slipping warms gently rather than alarming.

Component · the kernel’s voice

Suggestion

The coordinator proposes in place — never a chatbot, never a prompt box. Accept, adjust, or dismiss with a flick. Every suggestion carries its “because,” and every accepted one is reversible.

You’re almost out of oat milk. Add it to Saturday’s shop?
Because you buy it about every nine days, and the last one opened on the 4th.
You’ve okayed this a few times. Want me to just handle reordering from now on?
I’ll leave a receipt each time, and you can take it back whenever.

Component · the soul surface

Balance & members

Fairness is felt, not scored. Load renders as an ambient warm↔cool field that tilts gently — no leaderboard, no numbers-first. Capacity rings show who has room and who’s stretched, so work routes to the right person quietly.

Component · whole-surface state

Modes

The household is a state machine. On the hard weeks, a Mode reconfigures how work is distributed and surfaced — the world gently re-tints, load sheds, and notifications quiet. Grace under chaos, engineered.
